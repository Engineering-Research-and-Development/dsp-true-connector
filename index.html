System Overview

The DSP TRUE ("TRU"sted "E"ngineering) Connector is an open-source solution developed by ENG that makes data sharing simple, secure, and trustworthy. Fully certified in 2024 by the International Data Spaces Association (IDSA) it is now refactored to be compliant with Dataspace Protocol 2025-1, giving organizations a reliable way to exchange data with full control and in compliance with regulations such as GDPR.

Originally built for the manufacturing sector, the DSP TRUE Connector has since proven its strength across a wide range of domains. From energy management and smart grids to healthcare data exchange, agriculture, and critical infrastructure, wherever data sovereignty and secure interoperability are non-negotiable requirements, the connector delivers.

Its user-friendly interface makes all capabilities accessible to both technical users and business operators, no deep expertise required. The DSP TRUE Connector sits at the center of every data sharing interaction, orchestrating the full lifecycle between Data Providers and Data Consumers, whether operated by a human, an external service, or an AI Agent.

Compliance & Certification

Recognized by the Global Data Space Community

Interoperability in a data space is only valuable if it can be objectively verified. The DSP TRUE Connector does not just claim protocol compliance.

Listed in the IDSA Data Space Connector Report

The DSP TRUE Connector is one of only six connectors worldwide listed in the official IDSA Data Space Connector Report as fully compliant with Dataspace Protocol 2025-1. This report is the definitive reference for organizations evaluating data space connectors, published and maintained by the International Data Spaces Association.

Being listed here is not a marketing claim. It is a certification backed by the results of the DSP TCK (Technology Compatibility Kit), the official test suite that verifies 100% compliance with the protocol. Any organization adopting the DSP TRUE Connector is guaranteed out-of-the-box interoperability with every other DSP-compliant connector on the market.

AI-Ready Architecture

The Connector That Talks to Machines and Agents

Traditional data space connectors were designed around a human operator: browse, negotiate, approve, download. The DSP TRUE Connector takes a significant step beyond this model. With automated contract negotiation and multitenancy support, the connector becomes a first-class participant in programmatic and AI-driven workflows.

An AI Agent can now take the place of the human operator, browsing catalogs, negotiating contracts, and initiating transfers entirely on its own, governed by pre-configured policies. This opens the connector to a new class of integrations: autonomous data pipelines, AI services that pull fresh data on demand, and multi-agent architectures where different services negotiate and exchange data without human intervention at each step.

This shifts the data space from a tool for human operators to an infrastructure for intelligent services and significantly expands the business scenarios where the DSP TRUE Connector adds value.

Identity & Security

Enterprise-Grade Trust, End to End

Security in a data space is not just about encrypting the wire. It means verifying who is on the other side of every interaction, enforcing access policies at the protocol level, and maintaining a full audit record of every event. The DSP TRUE Connector handles all of this and does so using internationally recognized standards.

Keycloak Integration

The connector integrates with Keycloak for identity and access management, enabling enterprise-grade authentication and role-based access control. Organizations can plug in their existing identity infrastructure without having to redesign how users and services are managed.

Decentralized Claims Protocol (DCP)

The DSP TRUE Connector implements DCP, the decentralized identity standard developed within the IDSA community, allowing participants to assert and verify claims without relying on a central authority. The DCP integration is backed by its own TCK compliance suite, providing the same level of interoperability assurance at the identity layer that the DSP TCK provides at the protocol layer.

GDPR-Ready by Design

The contract negotiation mechanism is precisely the tool that makes GDPR-compliant data sharing achievable in practice. No data is transferred without an explicit, digitally enforced agreement. The audit trail captures every step, providing the evidentiary record that compliance requires.

How It Works

The Data Sharing Lifecycle

Every data exchange in the DSP TRUE Connector follows a well-defined, protocol-enforced sequence. This is not just a convention, it is a guarantee of interoperability with any other DSP-compliant connector on the market.

Step 1 — Discover

Browse the Catalog

The consumer points the Catalog Browser to a provider's endpoint. The connector fetches the DCAT-AP catalog, displays available datasets with their metadata, associated policies, and available distribution formats. No prior knowledge of the provider's infrastructure is needed.

Step 2 — Negotiate

Agree on Terms

The consumer selects a dataset and initiates a contract negotiation. The DSP protocol manages the full lifecycle — offer, request, agreement, finalization. Both sides can track the negotiation state in real time. Automated negotiation allows this step to happen without human intervention when policies permit.

Step 3 — Transfer

Exchange the Data

Once an agreement is in place, the consumer requests a transfer. The connector validates the authorization against the agreement, manages the delivery, and notifies the consumer when the artifact is ready for download. Push and pull transfer modes are both supported.

Step 4 — Audit

Full Traceability

Every step, from the first catalog browse to the final artifact download, is logged in the Audit Trail. Events are timestamped, attributed, and searchable. This complete record supports compliance reporting, security investigations, and operational monitoring without any additional tooling.
